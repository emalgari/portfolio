02 — About

A quiet
partner
with sharp tools.

My handle is emalgari — the "e" for electronic, "malgari" for companion. That's what I aim to be: the quiet person in the room who already solved the problem before you finished describing it.

I came to frontend from teaching physics, which explains a lot. I think about inertia, timing, and weight the way most developers think about frameworks. I care about accessibility and performance not because a linter said so, but because someone somewhere is using what I built on a slow connection.

These days I work mostly with React, Next.js, and TypeScript. When motion needs to earn its place I reach for GSAP. When it needs to feel alive I reach for Three.js. When it just needs to feel premium, I reach for Lenis.
