01 — La Maison

A maison devoted to the decorative stone.

Aurelle was founded in 1996 on a stubborn belief — that the ornamental stones of ancient jewellery deserve the same reverence as any diamond. Malachite banded like a forest at dusk. Lapis flecked with pyrite, like a night sky over the Rhône.

We cut them as cabochons, never calibrate them, and set them in recycled 18k gold with the patience of the old Lyon silk-weavers. Eight pieces a year. No seasons, no haste, no two stones alike.

— Hélène Aurelle, founder & lapidary

04 — Savoir-Faire

Four hands, one stone, a hundred hours.

Every Aurelle piece passes through exactly four pairs of hands — sourcer, lapidary, goldsmith, finisher. Nothing is outsourced; nothing is hurried.

01

The Sourcing

We buy at the mine, not the market. Each lot of malachite, lapis, opal and turquoise is traced, numbered and accompanied by its origin papers before it ever reaches Lyon.

02

The Cutting

Our lapidary cuts every cabochon by hand, reading the stone's bands and fire before the wheel ever turns. Nothing is calibrated; everything is composed.

03

The Setting

Gold is recycled, cast and raised at the bench. Bezels are closed by thumb and burnisher — no glue, no shortcuts, no prongs where a stone deserves a cradle.

04

The Finishing

Each piece is polished for six hours, hallmarked and sealed with its certificate. It leaves the atelier only when it could be inherited twice.

✦

18k Fairmined Gold

Recycled and fully traceable, alloyed in-house for a warmer, deeper yellow.

◆

Certified Natural Stones

No imitations, no undisclosed treatments. Origin papers accompany every piece.

❖

Lifetime Care

Re-polishing, re-setting and re-stringing for as long as the piece lives. Forever, we hope.
